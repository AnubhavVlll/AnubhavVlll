## About Me

AI systems are being deployed faster than organisations can govern — or deliver — them safely. I close both gaps at once.

I'm a Business Analyst managing the delivery of AI-native product features end-to-end: scoping requirements, sequencing sprints across engineering, legal, and executive stakeholders, and embedding risk and compliance checkpoints directly into the delivery pipeline — not bolted on after launch.

My approach is practical, not theoretical: I translate **NIST AI RMF** and **EU AI Act** requirements into sprint-level delivery constraints, build bias validation checkpoints into data pipelines, and manage cross-functional teams to ship automated decision systems that stay auditable, explainable, and compliant — on schedule.

Currently targeting roles in:

- 🛡️ **AI Governance** — Governance Analyst, Responsible AI Consultant
